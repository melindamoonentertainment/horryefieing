What Is A Damnable?

A Damnable is a living, dead, or in rare circumstances non-biological host that has been possessed by a Shedim spirit known as a Mazzik.

Unlike ordinary possession, where a spirit influences or controls its host, Mazzik possession causes a complete transformation of the vessel from within.

Once inside a suitable host, the Mazzik begins rewriting the structure of that being. In biological creatures, particularly humans, this process alters the host at a genetic level, gradually replacing the original form with one corrupted by the possessing spirit.

A Damnable is not an undead creature or a mindless monster. It is a demon inhabiting a physical vessel, retaining intelligence, personality and malicious intent.

The body may once have belonged to a living person, but the entity controlling it is the Mazzik spirit within.

The Damnable Process

The creation of a Damnable begins when a Mazzik successfully enters and possesses a host.

While almost any vessel may be affected under extreme circumstances, living biological hosts provide the strongest connection between the spirit and the physical world.

As the possession continues, the Mazzik begins altering the host from within. The body gradually becomes less like its original form and increasingly shaped by the will of the possessing entity.

During the earliest stages, the host may still retain some connection to its original self. However, as the transformation progresses, the influence of the Mazzik becomes stronger and recovery becomes increasingly difficult.

Stages Of Transformation

The transformation into a Damnable occurs gradually as the Mazzik spirit increases its control over the host. The process can be divided into several recognised stages.

Stage One - Possession

During the earliest stage, the Mazzik has entered the host but has not yet completely altered the vessel.

The original individual may still retain awareness and the connection between the host and the possessing spirit may still be disrupted through appropriate means.

However, the Mazzik immediately begins working to establish a stronger connection with the physical and spiritual aspects of the host.

Stage Two - Corruption And Mutation

As possession continues, the Mazzik begins rewriting the host from within. Biological structures are altered and the physical form gradually begins reflecting the influence of the possessing spirit.

At this stage, the host begins becoming a mutated Damnable. The original body and identity become increasingly intertwined with the corruption caused by the Mazzik.

Stage Three - Complete Manifestation

The final stage occurs when the transformation has progressed so far that the Mazzik no longer requires the original host identity to maintain control.

The vessel has become a physical manifestation of the Shedim spirit, with the original being absorbed into the rewritten form.

The Kelipah And The Divine Spark

The corruption of a host is not limited to the physical body. The Mazzik also attacks the spiritual connection within the individual.

During the earliest stages of possession, the Mazzik begins forming a kelipah around the divine spark contained within the human soul.

Because the Mazzik exists within shadow, the presence of the divine light within a soul acts as a barrier against complete possession. The kelipah serves to surround and obscure that light, allowing the darkness of the possessing spirit to spread.

As the corruption grows, the connection between the host and the divine spark becomes weaker. The greater the darkness becomes, the more control the Mazzik gains over the body and spirit.

Once the divine spark has been completely consumed and the light within the soul is extinguished, the host reaches the point where restoration is no longer possible.

At this stage, the Mazzik has not merely taken control of the host. It has claimed the vessel completely.

The Lost Host

One of the greatest dangers of a Damnable is the connection between the possessing spirit and the identity of the original host.

A Damnable may appear to be someone familiar, but the being within is not the person who was lost. It is a Mazzik spirit using the remains of that identity as a weapon.

Mazzikim may use memories, voices, appearances and emotional connections belonging to the host in order to deceive and manipulate others.

This makes encounters especially dangerous, as victims may hesitate when confronted with something wearing the form of someone they once knew.

The face may belong to a loved one, but the intelligence behind it belongs to the entity controlling the vessel.

Behaviour And Intelligence

Damnables are highly intelligent entities and should never be mistaken for mindless undead creatures.

The Mazzik within retains its own awareness, personality and malicious intent. It understands fear, emotion and weakness, allowing it to manipulate its victims rather than simply attack them.

Many Damnables display cruel and sadistic behaviour. They may prolong encounters, frighten their prey and deliberately exploit emotional vulnerabilities.

Their actions can resemble those of a spiteful child playing with something it has power over. The suffering of their victims is often part of the experience rather than merely a means to an end.

The cruelty of a Damnable does not come from the host. It comes from the Mazzik spirit controlling the body.

Physical Resilience

Once a host has become a Damnable, it no longer follows the limitations of ordinary biological life.

The Mazzik spirit sustains and controls the corrupted vessel, allowing the body to continue functioning despite damage that would be fatal to any normal organism.

Damnables possess extreme resilience and can survive catastrophic physical destruction. Loss of limbs, severe bodily damage and destruction of vital organs are not enough to guarantee the entity's defeat.

In advanced cases, a Damnable may continue functioning without a head or with multiple parts of its body destroyed. The physical form is merely the vessel; the true source of the entity's existence is the Mazzik spirit within.

Some Damnables have also demonstrated the ability to move beyond normal physical restrictions, including levitation and unnatural movement.

Supernatural Abilities

The abilities displayed by Damnables vary depending on the host and the individual Mazzik spirit responsible for the possession.

However, all Damnables possess abilities far beyond normal living creatures due to the connection between the physical vessel and the supernatural entity controlling it.

Commonly observed abilities include:

• Extreme physical strength.

• Enhanced endurance and resistance to injury.

• Levitation and unnatural movement.

• Ability to speak and communicate intelligently.

• Manipulation and psychological intimidation.

• Supernatural adaptation of the host body.

The greatest danger posed by a Damnable is not simply its physical strength, but the intelligence and cruelty of the Mazzik controlling it.

Weaknesses And Recovery

The possibility of saving a possessed host depends entirely on how far the transformation has progressed.

During the earliest stages of possession, the host may still be separated from the Mazzik before permanent changes occur.

As the corruption advances and the kelipah surrounding the divine spark grows stronger, the chances of recovery become increasingly limited.

Once the soul has been consumed and the original identity has been absorbed into the rewritten form, the host can no longer be restored.

Destroying the physical body alone is not always enough to defeat a Damnable. Effective methods depend on the individual Mazzik spirit and the connection it maintains with its vessel.
