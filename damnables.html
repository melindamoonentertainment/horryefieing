What Is A Damnable?

A Damnable is a living, dead, or in rare circumstances non-biological host that has been possessed by a Shedim spirit known as a Mazikin.

Unlike ordinary possession, where a spirit simply influences or controls a host, Mazikin possession causes a complete transformation of the host from within.

Once inside the host, the Mazikin begins rewriting the structure of the being it has possessed. In biological creatures, particularly humans, this process alters the host's DNA and gradually replaces the original form with something new.

A Damnable is not a zombie or a mindless creature. The entity controlling the body is an intelligent and malicious supernatural being with its own thoughts, desires and intentions.

The Damnable Process

The creation of a Damnable begins when a Mazikin spirit successfully enters a suitable host.

While almost any vessel can potentially become affected, living biological hosts provide the most effective connection between the physical world and the possessing spirit.

As the possession continues, the Mazikin gradually rewrites the host from within. The original form becomes corrupted as the spirit's influence spreads throughout the body.

The longer the possession remains active, the less likely the host is to be recovered. Eventually, the transformation reaches a point where the original identity of the host has been consumed by the changes taking place within them.

Stages Of Transformation

The transformation from possessed host to full Damnable does not happen instantly. The process occurs gradually as the Mazikin spirit continues to alter and consume the original form of its host.

Stage One - Possession

During the earliest stage, the Mazikin has successfully entered the host but the original being may still retain some control.

At this point, separation between the host and the possessing spirit may still be possible. However, the longer the Mazikin remains inside the host, the stronger its influence becomes.

Stage Two - Mutation

As the Mazikin continues rewriting the host, physical and biological changes begin to occur.

The host enters the mutated Damnable stage, where the original structure of the body has been significantly altered by the possessing spirit.

At this point, recovery becomes extremely unlikely, as the host's original biological identity has become intertwined with the Mazikin's corruption.

Stage Three - Complete Manifestation

The final stage occurs when the Mazikin has completely transformed the host into a physical manifestation of the Shedim spirit.

The host is no longer simply being controlled. The body itself has become a vessel created by the possessing entity.

The result is a fully realised Damnable: a physical form belonging to the Mazikin rather than the person or object that was originally possessed.

The Lost Host

One of the most dangerous aspects of a Damnable is the relationship between the possessing spirit and the identity of its host.

A Damnable is not the person it appears to be. The original individual may have once been a friend, family member or someone known to the victim, but the entity controlling the body is the Mazikin.

Mazikin spirits are capable of using the memories, appearance and identity of their hosts as tools of manipulation.

They may imitate the person they have possessed in order to create confusion, fear or emotional distress. This makes encounters with Damnables especially dangerous, as victims may hesitate to defend themselves against something wearing a familiar face.

The appearance of the host is only a disguise. Beneath it is a malicious intelligence using the remains of another being as a vessel.

Behaviour And Intelligence

Damnables are highly intelligent entities and should never be mistaken for mindless undead creatures.

The Mazikin spirit within the host retains its own awareness, personality and malicious intent. Rather than simply hunting for survival, many Damnables appear to take pleasure in manipulating and tormenting their victims.

Their behaviour is often cruel and unpredictable, similar to a spiteful child playing with something it has power over. They may prolong encounters, exploit emotional weaknesses and deliberately create fear before attacking.

This sadistic nature comes from the possessing spirit itself. The host body is merely the instrument through which the Mazikin expresses its intentions.

Physical Resilience

A Damnable host no longer follows the normal limitations of biological life. Once the Mazikin has taken sufficient control, the body becomes sustained by supernatural forces rather than ordinary anatomy.

Because of this, Damnables display extreme resistance to physical damage. Injuries that would be fatal to normal organisms often fail to stop them.

A Damnable may continue functioning after suffering catastrophic damage, including the loss of multiple limbs or severe destruction of the body.

In advanced cases, a Damnable does not require a head or conventional organs to remain active. The Mazikin spirit is the true source of the entity's continued existence, not the original biological systems of the host.

Even separated portions of a Damnable's body may continue to display unnatural movement or activity, demonstrating that these entities operate by supernatural rather than biological rules.

Supernatural Abilities

The abilities of a Damnable are created through the connection between the physical host and the possessing Mazikin spirit.

Although abilities vary depending on the host and the individual spirit involved, Damnables commonly display enhanced physical strength, unnatural endurance and abilities beyond human capability.

Many Damnables are capable of levitation, allowing them to move without being restricted by normal physical limitations.

They are also capable of speech and intelligent communication, allowing them to deceive, threaten and manipulate those around them.

Their greatest ability, however, is their intelligence. A Damnable understands fear, emotion and weakness, making them far more dangerous than a simple physical threat.

Weaknesses And Recovery

The greatest weakness of a Damnable is the transformation process itself.

During the early stages of possession, there remains a possibility that the host may be separated from the Mazikin spirit before permanent changes occur.

However, once the host reaches advanced mutation, the original form has been altered so severely that recovery becomes increasingly unlikely.

A fully manifested Damnable represents the final stage of transformation, where the Mazikin has become permanently connected to the physical form.

Methods of defeating a Damnable vary depending on the possessing spirit and the nature of the host. Extreme caution is required, as physical destruction alone may not be enough to stop the entity.
