Classification Archive

Entity Classification: Damnable

Damnables are a class of hostile extra-dimensional entities originating from the Shadow Realm. Unlike lesser manifestations or temporary demonic influences, Damnables possess independent consciousness, physical form and advanced intelligence.

These entities represent some of the most dangerous beings encountered by humanity, possessing unique abilities, enhanced physical capabilities and a natural connection to the dark forces that exist beyond the mortal plane.

Although Damnables share common characteristics, each species possesses its own individual traits, abilities and methods of interaction with the physical world.

Origins

The true origin of the Damnables remains one of the greatest mysteries studied by The Circle. Ancient records suggest that these beings originate from the Shadow Realm, a dimension existing beyond normal space and time.

Unlike traditional demons described in human mythology, Damnables are not simply spirits seeking possession. They are fully formed entities capable of existing independently within their own realm before entering the physical world.

Their connection to the Shadow Realm allows them to survive conditions impossible for ordinary lifeforms and grants them abilities beyond human understanding.

Creation Of A Damnable

The process by which a Damnable comes into existence is poorly understood, even among the most experienced members of The Circle. Unlike creatures that reproduce through natural means, Damnables are believed to be created through a combination of Shadow Realm energies and unknown supernatural processes.

Ancient records suggest that the formation of a Damnable requires a significant concentration of dark energy, allowing a new consciousness to develop and manifest into physical form.

While the exact method varies between species, every Damnable appears to share a connection to the same source of power that flows through the Shadow Realm.

Because of this, attempts to recreate or control the creation process have historically resulted in catastrophic consequences.

Physical Characteristics

Damnables display a wide range of physical forms, reflecting the unique nature of each species. While some may resemble distorted versions of living creatures, others possess completely alien appearances unlike anything found within the natural world.

Despite their differences, most Damnables share several common traits. Their bodies are adapted for survival in extreme environments, allowing them to withstand injuries that would be fatal to ordinary organisms.

Many possess enhanced strength, unnatural durability and sensory abilities far beyond human capability. Some species also demonstrate the ability to alter their appearance or adapt their physical form when entering the mortal realm.

The true anatomy of a Damnable remains largely unknown, as recovered remains often decay rapidly or return to Shadow Realm matter after death.

Behaviour And Intelligence

Contrary to early assumptions, Damnables are not mindless creatures driven purely by instinct. Most demonstrate advanced intelligence, strategic thinking and an understanding of their surroundings.

Their behaviour varies greatly between species. Some act as solitary predators, while others display complex social structures and forms of communication.

Damnables are capable of learning from encounters with humans and adapting their methods of attack. This intelligence makes them significantly more dangerous than lesser supernatural entities.

Researchers from The Circle have recorded instances of Damnables manipulating human fears, exploiting weaknesses and using deception rather than relying solely on physical force.

Abilities And Powers

Damnables possess abilities that separate them from ordinary lifeforms. These powers vary between species, but all originate from their connection to the Shadow Realm and the dark energies that exist within it.

Common abilities observed among Damnables include enhanced physical strength, accelerated healing, resistance to conventional weapons and the ability to survive environments impossible for human life.

Some Damnables have demonstrated additional supernatural abilities, including manipulation of energy, control over elements, unnatural movement, psychic abilities and influence over the minds of weaker beings.

Because every species develops differently, researchers consider each encounter with a Damnable to be unpredictable and potentially unique.

Weaknesses And Countermeasures

Despite their considerable power, Damnables are not invincible. Historical records maintained by The Circle show that every species possesses weaknesses that can be exploited.

These weaknesses differ depending on the individual entity, but commonly involve specific materials, supernatural forces, environmental conditions or methods of disrupting their connection to the Shadow Realm.

Direct confrontation is strongly discouraged. The Circle recommends careful observation, preparation and the use of specialised equipment when dealing with any confirmed Damnable presence.

Improper attempts at containment have frequently resulted in severe casualties.

Known Encounters

Throughout history, sightings of Damnables have been recorded across various locations and cultures. Many early accounts were dismissed as mythology, superstition or folklore.

Modern investigations conducted by The Circle have confirmed that many of these legends describe genuine encounters with Shadow Realm entities.

Every confirmed encounter provides further information about Damnables, allowing researchers to better understand their abilities, behaviour and methods of interaction with the mortal world.
