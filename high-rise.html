Horryefieing: High-Rise

High-Rise was released on October 27th, 2024 in time for Halloween and was released as a bonus game for patient fans. Despite starting out as a mini-game (originally marketted as a prologue for the upcoming "Circle Mansion") it went on to become a full game in itself through various updates and a v1.4 update (which is said to expand the game drastically) still to come
