Meet the Monsters of Rainford Court
Town Crier's Guide to Rainford's Monster Threat
CONTINUING ITS INVESTIGATION into the disturbing events surrounding Rainford Court, the Town Crier has assembled this guide to the creatures reportedly encountered within the building. Their origins remain uncertain, and many of the stories circulating among residents are difficult to verify. Nevertheless, our reporters have gathered accounts, photographs and eyewitness testimony concerning several of the more unusual figures said to inhabit the premises. What follows is our attempt to separate fact from rumour.:


Damnable: Your former neighbours after being possessed by the Mazzikim. Do not be fooled. They are no regular zombies. Damnables have sentience, supernatural strength and otherworldy abilities. And they are everywhere in Rainford Court.

Roshdam: Some Damnables, if in a more advanced stage of possession may be able to survive dismemberment to a point that they can control limbs individually. The Roshdam is the decapitated head of such things, growing legs from gore and continuing its attack as it bites at your ankles.

Flitterbug: These bloodsucking parasites are low level denizens of the Shadow Realm. Primarily bottom-feeders, they lure their prey by shape-shifting when solo and attack in swarms when in a pack. They are week but sheer numbers can really ruin your day.

Shedim: Shedim are the main force leading the attack on Rainford Court. They are entities that have no physical form of their own and can only obtain by possessing physical lifeforms and rewriting their DNA, gradually transforming the host into a physical manifestation of the entity inside. They are strong, deadly, highly intelligent and love to torment and play with their prey.

Z'vaah: The strongest of the demonic forces attacking Rainford Court, the Z'vaah is a large bullet sponge with the ability to teleport and throw devasting barrages of energy balls They have been known to wipe out entire resitences.
